Extra:

- Usage: - ❌ `Why I don't see you save the words?`
  - ✅ `Why don't I see you saving the words?`
  - ❌ `Why you didn't update the flashcards?`
  - ✅ `Why didn't you update the flashcards?`
- Examples:
  - _`Why don't I see the latest git commits in the repository?`_ (Tại sao tôi không thấy các commit git mới nhất trong repository?)
  - _`Why didn't the CI pipeline trigger on the pull request?`_ (Tại sao pipeline CI không kích hoạt khi có pull request?)
