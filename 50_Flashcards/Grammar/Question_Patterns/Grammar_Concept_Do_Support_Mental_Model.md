What is the first-principles mechanic of **Do-Support** in English questions and negatives?

---

- **The Split Mechanic:** In English, lexical main verbs cannot carry negation or invert to form questions on their own.
- **Workload Division:**
  1. The dummy auxiliary verb **'do / does / did'** absorbs tense, person, and negation (_'Did you...?'_, _'He doesn't...'_).
  2. The main lexical verb is stripped of all inflections and returns to its **Bare Infinitive ($V_0$)** form (_'...receive the payload?'_).
