What are the core mechanics and rules for **Head Noun Extraction in Compound Noun Phrases**?

---

- **Right-Headed Rule**: Trong cụm danh từ ghép, từ **tận cùng bên phải** luôn là Head Noun (quyết định số ít/nhiều).
- **Phản chiếu 180°**: Ngược với tiếng Việt (_Chính sách dọn cache_ $\Longleftrightarrow$ _cache eviction policy_).

---

Extra:

- 3 Tests:
  1. "What is it fundamentally?": `cache eviction policy` fundamentally is a `policy`.
  2. Rightmost position: the rightmost noun is the boss.
  3. Preposition inversion: `the policy for cache eviction`.
- Examples:
  - `system architecture diagram` $\rightarrow$ `diagram` (singular $\rightarrow$ `Does / Is`).
  - `background worker threads` $\rightarrow$ `threads` (plural $\rightarrow$ `Do / Are`).
