What is the correct subject-verb agreement for **You** in past tense (You were / You didn't)?

---

- **Formula**: `To Be (Past)`: `You were...` / `You were not (weren't)...`; `Action Verb (Past)`: `You didn't + V-bare`
- **Core Usage**: - Với đại từ `You` (ngôi thứ 2), động từ `to be` ở quá khứ LUÔN dùng `were` (không dùng `was`). Phủ định hành động...

---

Extra:

- Usage: - ❌ `You was...` / ❌ `You was not activate...`
  - ✅ `You were...` / ✅ `You didn't activate...`
- Examples:
  - _`You were not in the office yesterday.`_ (Bạn đã không ở văn phòng hôm qua.)
  - _`You didn't activate the feature flag.`_ (Bạn đã không kích hoạt feature flag.)
