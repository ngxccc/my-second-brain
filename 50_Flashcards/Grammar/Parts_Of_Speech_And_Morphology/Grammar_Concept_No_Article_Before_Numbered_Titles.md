What is the article rule when using **the** before numbered titles (Lab 4, Chapter 2)?

---

- **Formula**: `[Noun] + [Number]` không dùng "the" (`in Lab 4`, `on Page 10`); `the + [Ordinal] + [Noun]` dùng "the" (`in the 4th lab`).
- **Core Usage**: Không dùng mạo từ "the" trước danh từ đi kèm số hiệu cụ thể (`Lab 4`, NOT `the Lab 4`).

---

Extra:

- Error comparison:
  - ❌ `in the lab 4` $\rightarrow$ ✅ `in Lab 4`
  - ❌ `on the page 10` $\rightarrow$ ✅ `on Page 10`
- Examples:
  - `Complete all tasks in Lab 4 before submitting.`
  - `Refer to the 4th lab in the course syllabus.`
