How do you align **Time Adverbs** (Currently, Lately/Recently, At that time) with English tenses in engineering reports?

---

- **Currently / At present:** Signals an ongoing action in progress $\rightarrow$ Align with **Present Continuous** or Presen...
- **Lately / Recently:** Signals an action completed within an open time frame connecting to the present $\rightarrow$ Align...

---

Extra:

- **At that time / Back then:** Signals a closed period in the past $\rightarrow$ Align with **Past Simple** (_'At that time, we used MySQL 5.7'_).
