What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **establish**?

---

- **Meaning**: To set up or create an organization, system, or rule on a firm or permanent basis.

- **Primary Collocation**: `establish` in technical context

---

Extra:

- Pronunciation: /ɪˈstæb.lɪʃ/
- Type: Verb
- Example: - _establishing strict end-to-end type safety_
