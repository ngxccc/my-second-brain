What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **strategic outlook**?

---

- **Meaning**: A high-level, forward-looking perspective that evaluates technical choices in terms of long-term business goals, scalability, and system longevity (tầm nhìn chi...
- **Primary Collocation**: `develop a strategic outlook` / `architectural strategic outlook`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /strəˈtiː.dʒɪk ˈaʊt.lʊk/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `develop a strategic outlook` (phát triển tầm nhìn chiến lược)
  - `architectural strategic outlook` (góc nhìn chiến lược về mặt kiến trúc)
  - `balance tactical fixes with a strategic outlook` (cân bằng sửa lỗi trước mắt với tầm nhìn chiến lược)
- Examples:
  - _Senior engineers stand out by combining rapid bug fixes with a broad **strategic outlook** on system debt._
  - _Adopting Go for microservices was part of the engineering team's five-year **strategic outlook**._
