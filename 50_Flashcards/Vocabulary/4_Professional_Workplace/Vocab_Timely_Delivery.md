What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **timely delivery**?

---

- **Meaning**: The act of delivering software or tasks punctually according to schedule.

- **Primary Collocation**: `timely delivery` in technical context

---

Extra:

- Pronunciation: /ˈtaɪm.li dɪˈlɪv.ər.i/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Example: - _timely delivery of assigned tasks_
