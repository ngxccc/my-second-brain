What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **utilize**?

---

- **Meaning**: To make practical and effective use of a resource or tool.

- **Primary Collocation**: `utilize` in technical context

---

Extra:

- Pronunciation: /ˈjuː.təl.aɪz/
- Type: Verb
- Example: - _utilizing React Server Components_
