What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **set the stage**?

---

- **Meaning**: To prepare conditions, provide foundational context, or establish background so that subsequent developments or explanations can succeed (tạ...
- **Primary Collocation**: `set the stage for discussion` / `set the stage for microservice migration`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /set ðə ˈsteɪdʒ/
- Type: Idiomatic Verb Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `set the stage for discussion` (chuẩn bị bối cảnh cho cuộc thảo luận)
  - `set the stage for microservice migration` (tạo tiền đề cho việc di chuyển sang microservice)
  - `a brief introduction sets the stage` (lời giới thiệu ngắn gọn tạo tiền đề cho buổi nói chuyện)
- Examples:
  - _Start your whiteboard presentation by stating the user scale; this **sets the stage** for architectural decisions._
  - _Rigorous unit testing **sets the stage** for confident automated deployments._
