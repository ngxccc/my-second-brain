What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **messing around with**?

---

- **Meaning**: Experimenting casually, tinkering with, or trying out a technology without a formal or strict plan (nghịch ngợm, vọc vạch thử nghiệm công nghệ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `mess around with a new framework` / `stop messing around with settings`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈmes.ɪŋ əˈraʊnd wɪð/
- Type: Idiomatic Phrasal Verb Expression
- Collocations:
  - `mess around with a new framework` (vọc vạch một framework mới)
  - `stop messing around with settings` (ngừng nghịch linh tinh các tùy chọn)
  - `mess around with local models` (thử nghiệm chạy các mô hình cục bộ)
- Examples:
  - _I was **messing around with** eBPF tracing tools to see kernel-level socket allocations._
  - _Don't deploy experimental configs to staging when you are just **messing around with** parameters._
