What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **cortisol**?

---

- **Meaning**: A steroid hormone produced by adrenal glands in response to stress and low blood glucose (hormone căng thẳng cortisol).
- **Primary Collocation**: `elevated cortisol levels` / `cortisol spike under pressure`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈkɔːr.t̬ə.sɑːl/
- Type: Noun
- Collocations:
  - `elevated cortisol levels` (nồng độ cortisol tăng cao)
  - `cortisol spike under pressure` (đột biến cortisol khi gặp áp lực)
  - `manage cortisol during interviews` (kiểm soát cortisol khi phỏng vấn)
- Examples:
  - _Deep breathing before a technical presentation lowers **cortisol** and restores cognitive clarity._
  - _Chronic sleep deprivation keeps **cortisol** high, impairing short-term memory retrieval._
