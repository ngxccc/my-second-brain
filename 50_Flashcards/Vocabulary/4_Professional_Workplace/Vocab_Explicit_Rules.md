What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **explicit rules**?

---

- **Meaning**: Clearly stated, unambiguous guidelines or invariants that leave no room for uncertainty, implication, or guesswork (các quy tắc tường minh, rõ ràng).
- **Primary Collocation**: `define explicit rules` / `follow explicit rules`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ɪkˈsplɪs.ɪt ˈruːlz/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `define explicit rules` (định nghĩa các quy tắc tường minh)
  - `follow explicit rules` (tuân thủ các quy tắc rõ ràng)
  - `enforce explicit rules in code reviews` (áp dụng các quy tắc tường minh khi duyệt mã)
- Examples:
  - _Go favours **explicit rules** over implicit magic, requiring explicit error checking for every fallible call._
  - _Having **explicit rules** for vault taxonomy prevents folder depth sprawl._
