What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **transition**?

---

- **Meaning**: The process or a period of changing from one state, stage, condition, or topic to another (sự chuyển đổi, chuyển tiếp; câu chuyển tiếp).
- **Primary Collocation**: `smooth transition` / `transition to microservices`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /trænˈzɪʃ.ən/
- Type: Noun / Verb (Word Family)
- Collocations:
  - `smooth transition` (sự chuyển tiếp mượt mà)
  - `transition to microservices` (chuyển dịch sang kiến trúc microservices)
  - `signposting transitions` (các câu chuyển ý định hướng người nghe trong thuyết trình)
- Examples:
  - _Noun: Using explicit signposting phrases creates a smooth **transition** between architecture diagrams._
  - _Verb: The team successfully **transitioned** from weekly manual deployments to automated continuous delivery._
