What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **visual cues**?

---

- **Meaning**: Sensory graphic signals, arrows, colors, or spatial markers that direct attention and convey meaning without relying solely on words (các dấu hiệu trực quan).
- **Primary Collocation**: `provide visual cues` / `rely on visual cues`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈvɪʒ.u.əl ˈkjuːz/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `provide visual cues` (cung cấp các dấu hiệu trực quan)
  - `rely on visual cues` (dựa vào các tín hiệu trực quan)
  - `effective visual cues on diagrams` (dấu hiệu trực quan hiệu quả trên sơ đồ)
- Examples:
  - _Drawing red arrows between services gives immediate **visual cues** indicating synchronous network calls._
  - _Using consistent colors provides clear **visual cues** that separate database reads from writes._
