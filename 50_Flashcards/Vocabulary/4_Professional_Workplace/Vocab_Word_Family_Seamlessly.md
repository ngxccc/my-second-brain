What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **seamlessly**?

---

- **Meaning**: Smoothly and continuously, without any noticeable gaps, problems, or awkward transitions (một cách mượt mà, liền mạch, không tì vết).
- **Primary Collocation**: `seamlessly combine` / `seamlessly integrate`

---

  - **Adjective**: `seamless` (/ˈsiːm.ləs/ - liền mạch, không đường nối)
