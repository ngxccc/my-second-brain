What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **engage the audience**?

---

- **Meaning**: To capture, hold, and maintain the interest and mental focus of listeners during a speech or presentation (thu hút và lôi cuốn người nghe).
- **Primary Collocation**: `how to engage the audience` / `engage the audience with questions`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒ ði ˈɔː.di.əns/
- Type: Verb Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `how to engage the audience` (cách thu hút khán giả)
  - `engage the audience with questions` (lôi cuốn người nghe bằng câu hỏi)
  - `effectively engage the audience` (thu hút người nghe một cách hiệu quả)
- Examples:
  - _Drawing diagrams live on a digital board is a proven tactic to **engage the audience**._
  - _Making eye contact and using natural open-palm gestures helps **engage the audience** during technical reviews._
