What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **deliver**?

---

- **Meaning**: To provide, produce, or yield a promised result, service, or experience (mang lại, cung cấp giải pháp/kết quả).
- **Primary Collocation**: `deliver a personalized experience` / `deliver value`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /dɪˈlɪv.ər/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `deliver` (/dɪˈlɪv.ər/ - mang lại, cung cấp)
  - **Noun**: `delivery` (/dɪˈlɪv.ər.i/ - sự cung cấp/sự giao hàng) / `deliverable` (/dɪˈlɪv.ər.ə.bəl/ - sản phẩm bàn giao)
  - **Adjective**: `deliverable` (/dɪˈlɪv.ər.ə.bəl/)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `deliver a personalized experience` (mang lại trải nghiệm cá nhân hóa)
  - `deliver value` (mang lại giá trị)
  - `deliver results` (mang lại kết quả)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Netflix AI **delivers** a hyper-personalized homepage to every subscriber._
  - _Noun: The **delivery** of real-time recommendations happens in milliseconds._
  - _Noun: Key **deliverables** of the project include the data pipeline and recommendation algorithm._
