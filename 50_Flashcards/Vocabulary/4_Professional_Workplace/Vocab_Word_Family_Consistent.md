What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **consistent**?

---

- **Meaning**: Always behaving, performing, or appearing in the same way; retaining visual or logical harmony (đồng bộ, nhất quán).
- **Primary Collocation**: `visual consistency` / `consistent design`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /kənˈsɪs.tənt/
- Type: Adjective (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `consistency` (/kənˈsɪs.tən.si/ - sự nhất quán, tính đồng bộ)
  - **Adjective**: `consistent` (/kənˈsɪs.tənt/ - đồng bộ, nhất quán)
  - **Adverb**: `consistently` (/kənˈsɪs.tənt.li/ - một cách nhất quán)
- Collocations:
  - `visual consistency` (sự đồng bộ về mặt thị giác)
  - `consistent design` (thiết kế nhất quán)
- Examples:
  - _Noun: Maintaining visual **consistency** across slides improves presentation quality._
  - _Adjective: Use a **consistent** icon set so line weights match on every card._
  - _Adverb: The recommendation algorithm performs **consistently** under heavy load._
