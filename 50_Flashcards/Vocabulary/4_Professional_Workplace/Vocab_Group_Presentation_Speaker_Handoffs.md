What are the two core phrases for Speaker Handoffs in group technical presentations?

---

- **Outgoing Speaker**: _"Now, I'll pass the mic to [Name] to walk you through [Topic]."_
- **Incoming Speaker**: _"Thank you, [Name]. Let's dive into [Topic]."_

---

Extra:

- Closer Pattern: _"To bring everything together, our conclusion is..."_
- Objective: Smooth handoffs prevent awkward silences and demonstrate polished teamwork.
