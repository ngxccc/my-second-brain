What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **wrap up**?

---

- **Meaning**: To bring an activity, presentation, meeting, or project to a neat, satisfying conclusion; to summarize final takeaways (tổng kết, kết thúc buổi trình bày).
- **Primary Collocation**: `wrap up the presentation` / `wrap up with a summary`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ræp ˈʌp/
- Type: Phrasal Verb
- Collocations:
  - `wrap up the presentation` (kết thúc bài thuyết trình)
  - `wrap up with a summary` (tổng kết bằng một bản tóm tắt)
  - `let's wrap up here` (chúng ta hãy kết thúc tại đây)
- Examples:
  - _To **wrap up** his whiteboard session, he highlighted the three core latency wins of the new architecture._
  - _We have five minutes remaining, so let us **wrap up** the design review and assign action items._
