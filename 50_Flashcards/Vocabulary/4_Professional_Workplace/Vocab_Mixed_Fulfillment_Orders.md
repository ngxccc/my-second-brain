Our order routing engine splits ~~mixed fulfillment orders~~ across the nearest regional hubs to minimize shipping cost.

---

Extra:

- Meaning: Customer orders that require items to be picked and dispatched from multiple different warehouses or fulfillment channels.
- Pronunciation: /ˌmɪkst fʊlˈfɪl.mənt ˌɔːr.dɚz/
- Collocations:
  - `route mixed fulfillment orders` (điều phối các đơn hàng xử lý hỗn hợp)
  - `process mixed fulfillment orders` (xử lý đơn hàng từ nhiều kho)
  - `split mixed fulfillment orders` (tách đơn hàng thành nhiều gói vận chuyển)
