What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **mixed fulfillment orders**?

---

- **Meaning**: Customer orders that require items to be picked and dispatched from multiple different warehouses or fulfillment channels (đơn hàng xử lý hỗ...
- **Primary Collocation**: `route mixed fulfillment orders` / `process mixed fulfillment orders`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌmɪkst fʊlˈfɪl.mənt ˌɔːr.dɚz/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `route mixed fulfillment orders` (điều phối các đơn hàng xử lý hỗn hợp)
  - `process mixed fulfillment orders` (xử lý đơn hàng từ nhiều kho)
  - `split mixed fulfillment orders` (tách đơn hàng thành nhiều gói vận chuyển)
- Examples:
  - _Our order routing engine splits **mixed fulfillment orders** across the nearest regional hubs to minimize shipping cost._
  - _Handling **mixed fulfillment orders** requires real-time inventory synchronization across all physical warehouses._
