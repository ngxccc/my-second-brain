What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **ensure**?

---

- **Meaning**: To make certain that something will occur or be the case.

- **Primary Collocation**: `ensure` in technical context

---

Extra:

- Pronunciation: /ɪnˈʃʊər/
- Type: Verb
- Example: - _to ensure B2B checkout data integrity_
