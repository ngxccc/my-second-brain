What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **First Principles Thinking**?

---

- **Meaning**: A problem-solving technique of breaking down a complex problem into its most basic, foundational truths and reasoning up from there.

- **Primary Collocation**: `First Principles Thinking` in technical context
