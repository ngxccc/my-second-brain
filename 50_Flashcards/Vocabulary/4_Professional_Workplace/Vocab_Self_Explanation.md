What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **self explanation**?

---

- **Meaning**: A metacognitive learning strategy where learners explain concepts, code flows, or logic to themselves out loud to verify comprehension (tự giải thích cơ chế cho...
- **Primary Collocation**: `practice self explanation` / `self explanation effect in learning`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌself ˌek.spləˈneɪ.ʃən/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `practice self explanation` (thực hành tự giải thích)
  - `self explanation effect in learning` (hiệu ứng tự giải thích trong học tập)
  - `use self explanation for debugging` (dùng kỹ thuật tự giải thích để tìm lỗi)
- Examples:
  - _Using **self explanation** while tracing a Go pointer reveals exactly where a memory leak originates._
  - _The Feynman technique is essentially structured **self explanation** using simple language._
