What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **cuts through the fluff**?

---

- **Meaning**: To get directly to the essential, practical point while discarding unnecessary, superficial details or corporate buzzwords (đi thẳng vào trọng tâm, loại bỏ râu...
- **Primary Collocation**: `cut through the fluff` / `RFC that cuts through the fluff`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /kʌts θruː ðə flʌf/
- Type: Idiomatic Verb Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `cut through the fluff` (đi thẳng vào thực chất)
  - `RFC that cuts through the fluff` (tài liệu thiết kế đi thẳng vào vấn đề cốt lõi)
  - `cut through marketing fluff` (loại bỏ từ ngữ tiếp thị rỗng tuếch)
- Examples:
  - _A good architecture review **cuts through the fluff** and focuses strictly on throughput and memory latency._
  - _His presentation style **cuts through the fluff**, delivering high signal in under ten minutes._
