What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **timeframe**?

---

- **Meaning**: A specified period of time during which something occurs or is planned to take place (khoảng thời gian / khung thời gian).
- **Primary Collocation**: `case study timeframe` / `specified timeframe`
