What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **auditory feedback**?

---

- **Meaning**: The sensory hearing perception of one's own voice and articulation that the brain uses to monitor and adjust speech in real time (phản hồi thính giác khi phát âm).
- **Primary Collocation**: `rely on auditory feedback` / `auditory feedback loop`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈɔː.də.tɔːr.i ˈfiːd.bæk/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `rely on auditory feedback`
  - `auditory feedback loop`
  - `bone conduction auditory feedback`
- Examples:
  - _Bone conduction provides clear **auditory feedback** even when you whisper presentation scripts._
