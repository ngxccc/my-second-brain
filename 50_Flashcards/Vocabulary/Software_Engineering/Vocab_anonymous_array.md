What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **anonymous array**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /əˈnɑː.nə.məs əˈreɪ/
- **Meaning**: An unnamed, compiler-allocated fixed-size array created implicitly in memory to back a slice literal (mảng ẩn danh được Go tự động cấp phát bên dưới).
- **Collocations**:
  - `allocate an anonymous array`
  - `anonymous array on the heap`
  - `reference an anonymous array`
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _When initializing a slice with a literal `[]int{1, 2, 3}`, Go allocates an **anonymous array** under the hood._
