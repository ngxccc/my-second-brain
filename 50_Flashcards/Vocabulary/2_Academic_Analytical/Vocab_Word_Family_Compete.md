What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **compete**?

---

- **Meaning**: To strive to gain or win something by defeating or establishing superiority over others (cạnh tranh).
- **Primary Collocation**: `competitive advantage` / `stiff competition`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /kəmˈpiːt/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `compete` (/kəmˈpiːt/ - cạnh tranh)
  - **Noun**: `competition` (/ˌkɒm.pəˈtɪʃ.ən/ - sự cạnh tranh) / `competitor` (/kəmˈpet.ɪ.tər/ - đối thủ cạnh tranh)
  - **Adjective**: `competitive` (/kəmˈpet.ə.tɪv/ - mang tính cạnh tranh)
  - **Adverb**: `competitively` (/kəmˈpet.ə.tɪv.li/ - một cách cạnh tranh)
- Collocations:
  - `competitive advantage` (lợi thế cạnh tranh)
  - `stiff competition` (sự cạnh tranh gay gắt)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Tech companies **compete** for subscriber market share._
  - _Noun: AI algorithms provide a distinct **competition** edge._
  - _Adjective: Personalized artwork creates a sustainable **competitive** advantage._
  - _Adverb: Netflix prices its services **competitively** globally._
