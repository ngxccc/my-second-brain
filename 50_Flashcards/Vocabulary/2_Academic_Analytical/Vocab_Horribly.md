What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **horribly**?

---

- **Meaning**: In a very bad, shocking, or unpleasant manner; extremely poorly (một cách tồi tệ, kinh khủng).
- **Primary Collocation**: `fail horribly` / `go horribly wrong`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈhɔːr.ə.bli/
- Type: Adverb
- Collocations:
  - `fail horribly`
  - `go horribly wrong`
  - `horribly inefficient query`
- Examples:
  - _An unindexed database query performs **horribly** under high concurrent traffic._
