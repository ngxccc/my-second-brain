What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **in-depth**?

---

- **Meaning**: Done carefully and in great detail; thorough and comprehensive (chuyên sâu, toàn diện, thấu đáo).
- **Primary Collocation**: `in-depth analysis` / `in-depth knowledge`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌɪnˈdeptθ/
- Type: Adjective / Adverb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `deepen` (/ˈdiː.pən/ - làm sâu sắc hơn, đào sâu)
  - **Noun**: `depth` (/deptθ/ - độ sâu, chiều sâu)
  - **Adjective**: `in-depth` (/ˌɪnˈdeptθ/ - chuyên sâu, kỹ lưỡng), `deep` (/diːp/ - sâu)
  - **Adverb**: `deeply` (/ˈdiːp.li/ - một cách sâu sắc), `in-depth` (/ˌɪnˈdeptθ/ - theo cách chuyên sâu)
- Collocations:
  - `in-depth analysis` (phân tích chuyên sâu)
  - `in-depth knowledge` (kiến thức sâu rộng)
  - `in-depth debate` (cuộc tranh luận chuyên sâu/kỹ lưỡng)
- Examples:
  - _Adjective: The debate did not require an **in-depth** understanding of machine learning algorithms._
  - _Noun: The team explored the problem in **depth** before finalizing the system design._
  - _Verb: The training session helped engineers **deepen** their understanding of concurrency patterns._
