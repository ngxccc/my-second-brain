What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **latency**?

---

- **Meaning**: The delay before a transfer of data begins following an instruction (độ trễ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `latency spike` / `low latency`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈleɪ.tən.si/
- Type: Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `latency` (/ˈleɪ.tən.si/ - độ trễ)
  - **Adjective**: `latent` (/ˈleɪ.tənt/ - tiềm tàng, tiềm ẩn)
  - **Adverb**: `latently` (/ˈleɪ.tənt.li/ - một cách tiềm ẩn)
- Collocations:
  - `latency spike` (sự tăng vọt độ trễ)
  - `low latency` (độ trễ thấp)
- Examples:
  - _Noun: Go's Garbage Collector caused frequent **latency** spikes._
  - _Adjective: The system harbored **latent** performance bottlenecks._
