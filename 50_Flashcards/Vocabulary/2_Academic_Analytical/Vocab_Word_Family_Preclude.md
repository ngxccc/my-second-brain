What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **preclude**?

---

- **Meaning**: To prevent something from happening or make it impossible (ngăn ngừa, loại trừ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `preclude errors` / `preclude access`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /prɪˈkluːd/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `preclude` (/prɪˈkluːd/ - ngăn ngừa, loại trừ)
  - **Noun**: `preclusion` (/prɪˈkluː.ʒən/ - sự ngăn ngừa)
  - **Adjective**: `preclusive` (/prɪˈkluː.sɪv/ - mang tính ngăn ngừa)
  - **Adverb**: `preclusively` (/prɪˈkluː.sɪv.li/ - một cách ngăn ngừa)
- Collocations:
  - `preclude errors` (ngăn ngừa lỗi)
  - `preclude access` (loại trừ truy cập)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Strict typing **precludes** runtime null pointer exceptions._
  - _Noun: The **preclusion** of memory leaks improves uptime._
  - _Adjective: The policy acts as a **preclusive** measure against security breaches._
