What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **opt**?

---

- **Meaning**: To make a choice or decision from a range of possibilities (lựa chọn, chọn phương án).
- **Primary Collocation**: `opt for something` / `opt to do something`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ɒpt/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `opt` (/ɒpt/ - lựa chọn, chọn)
  - **Noun**: `option` (/ˈɒp.ʃən/ - lựa chọn, phương án)
  - **Adjective**: `optional` (/ˈɒp.ʃən.əl/ - tùy chọn, không bắt buộc)
  - **Adverb**: `optionally` (/ˈɒp.ʃən.əl.i/ - một cách tùy chọn)
- Collocations:
  - `opt for something` (chọn phương án nào đó)
  - `opt to do something` (chọn làm điều gì đó)
  - `configuration option` (tùy chọn cấu hình)
  - `optional parameter` (tham số không bắt buộc)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The tech lead decided to **opt for** a modular monolith structure._
  - _Noun: We evaluated each **option** based on latency and operational overhead._
  - _Adjective: Passing an authentication token is **optional** in public endpoints._
  - _Adverb: The CLI tool **optionally** outputs the report in JSON format._
