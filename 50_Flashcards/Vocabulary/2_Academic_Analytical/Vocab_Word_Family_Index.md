What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **index**?

---

- **Meaning**: A systematic list or indicator used for quick data lookup and organizing notes (chỉ mục / lập chỉ mục).
- **Primary Collocation**: `index data` / `automated indexing`

---

  - **Adjective**: `indexical` (/ɪnˈdek.sɪ.kəl/ - mang tính chỉ mục)
