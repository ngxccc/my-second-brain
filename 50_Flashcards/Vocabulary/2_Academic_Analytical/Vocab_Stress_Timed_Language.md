What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **stress-timed language**?

---

- **Meaning**: A language (like English) where the duration between stressed syllables is equal, regardless of how many unstressed syllables fall between them.

- **Primary Collocation**: `stress-timed language` in technical context
