Understanding ~~connected speech~~ like catenation and elision is essential for English listening comprehension.

---

Extra:

- Meaning: Spoken language in a continuous sequence where words link, merge, assimilate, or omit sounds compared to their isolated dictionary forms (ngữ lưu, chuỗi lời nói...
- Pronunciation: /kəˈnek.tɪd ˈspiːtʃ/
- Collocations:
  - `features of connected speech` (các đặc trưng của ngữ lưu nối âm)
  - `master connected speech` (làm chủ hiện tượng nối âm trong lời nói)
  - `connected speech phonetics` (ngữ âm học ngữ lưu)
