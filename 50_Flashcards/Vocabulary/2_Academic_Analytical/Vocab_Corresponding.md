What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **corresponding**?

---

- **Meaning**: Directly related, matching, or equivalent in character, form, or function (tương ứng, tương thích).
- **Primary Collocation**: `corresponding author/engineer` / `find the corresponding key`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌkɔːr.əˈspɑːn.dɪŋ/
- Type: Adjective (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `correspond` (/ˌkɔːr.əˈspɑːnd/ - tương ứng, trao đổi thư từ)
  - **Noun**: `correspondence` (/ˌkɔːr.əˈspɑːn.dəns/ - sự tương ứng / thư từ)
  - **Adjective**: `corresponding` (/ˌkɔːr.əˈspɑːn.dɪŋ/ - tương ứng)
  - **Adverb**: `correspondingly` (/ˌkɔːr.əˈspɑːn.dɪŋ.li/ - một cách tương ứng)
- Collocations:
  - `corresponding author/engineer` (kỹ sư tương ứng phụ trách)
  - `find the corresponding key` (tìm khóa tương ứng trong bảng băm)
  - `corresponding increase in latency` (sự tăng độ trễ tương ứng)
- Examples:
  - _Adjective: Each slice header stores a pointer to its **corresponding** underlying array._
  - _Adverb: As traffic tripled, memory consumption increased **correspondingly**._
