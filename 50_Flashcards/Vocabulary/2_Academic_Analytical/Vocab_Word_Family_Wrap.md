What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **wrap**?

---

- **Meaning**: To cover, enclose, or cause text to fit within screen margins automatically (tự động xuống dòng / bao bọc).
- **Primary Collocation**: `word wrap` / `soft wrapping`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ræp/
- Type: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `wrap` (/ræp/ - xuống dòng, bao bọc)
  - **Noun**: `wrapper` (/ˈræp.ər/ - lớp bọc) / `wrapping` (/ˈræp.ɪŋ/ - việc xuống dòng/bọc)
  - **Adjective**: `wrapped` (/ræpt/)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `word wrap` (tự động xuống dòng chữ)
  - `soft wrapping` (việc xuống dòng mềm theo kích thước màn hình)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Removing code blocks allows text to **wrap** dynamically on mobile devices._
  - _Noun: Obsidian's **word wrap** feature enhances script readability._
  - _Adjective: The **wrapped** Markdown text fits cleanly within the editor margins._
