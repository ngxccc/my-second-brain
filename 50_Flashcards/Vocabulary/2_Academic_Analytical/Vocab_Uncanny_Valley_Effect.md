What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **Uncanny Valley Effect**?

---

- **Meaning**: A phenomenon where something that looks or sounds almost natural—but slightly off—causes feelings of discomfort or unnaturalness.

- **Primary Collocation**: `Uncanny Valley Effect` in technical context
