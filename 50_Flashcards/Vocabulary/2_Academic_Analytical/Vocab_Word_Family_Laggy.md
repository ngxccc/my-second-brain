What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **laggy**?

---

- **Meaning**: Experiencing a delay or slow response time (bị giật lag, trễ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `laggy response` / `network lag`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈlæɡ.i/
- Type: Adjective (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `lag` (/læɡ/ - giật, trễ, đi chậm hơn)
  - **Noun**: `lag` (/læɡ/ - độ trễ, sự trễ)
  - **Adjective**: `laggy` (/ˈlæɡ.i/ - bị giật lag)
  - **Adverb**: `laggily` (/ˈlæɡ.ɪ.li/ - một cách giật lag)
- Collocations:
  - `laggy response` (phản hồi giật lag)
  - `network lag` (độ trễ mạng)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Response time began to **lag** during GC cycles._
  - _Noun: High **lag** degrades the user experience._
  - _Adjective: The Read States service became **laggy** under high traffic._
  - _Adverb: The game rendered **laggily** on outdated hardware._
