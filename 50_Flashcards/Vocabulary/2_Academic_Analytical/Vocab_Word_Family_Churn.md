What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **churn**?

---

- **Meaning**: The rate at which customers stop subscribing to a service; to lose customers (tỷ lệ hủy dịch vụ / rời bỏ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `churn rate` / `reduce churn`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /tʃɜːn/
- Type: Noun / Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `churn` (/tʃɜːn/ - rời bỏ / làm xáo trộn)
  - **Noun**: `churn` (/tʃɜːn/ - tỷ lệ hủy dịch vụ) / `churner` (/ˈtʃɜː.nər/ - khách hàng rời bỏ)
  - **Adjective**: `churning` (/ˈtʃɜː.nɪŋ/ - thuộc về xáo trộn/hủy)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `churn rate` (tỷ lệ hủy dịch vụ)
  - `reduce churn` (giảm tỷ lệ hủy dịch vụ)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: High latency causes users to **churn** to competitor platforms._
  - _Noun: AI recommendations significantly reduce the monthly **churn** rate._
  - _Adjective: The company implemented strategies to combat **churning** subscribers._
