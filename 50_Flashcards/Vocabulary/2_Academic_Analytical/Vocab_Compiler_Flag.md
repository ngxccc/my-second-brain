What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **compiler flag**?

---

- **Meaning**: A command-line switch or parameter passed to a compiler to control optimization, debugging output, or compilation behavior (cờ biên dịch).
- **Primary Collocation**: `pass a compiler flag` / `optimization compiler flag`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /kəmˈpaɪ.lɚ ˌflæɡ/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `pass a compiler flag` (truyền cờ biên dịch)
  - `optimization compiler flag` (cờ biên dịch tối ưu hóa)
  - `inspect escape analysis via compiler flags` (kiểm tra phân tích thoát qua cờ biên dịch)
- Examples:
  - _Use the **compiler flag** `-gcflags="-m"` in Go to inspect inlining and escape analysis decisions._
  - _Enabling the `-Wall` **compiler flag** surfaces all subtle compiler warnings._
