What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **cite**?

---

- **Meaning**: To quote or refer to a source, book, or authority as evidence for an argument (trích dẫn).
- **Primary Collocation**: `source citation` / `cite evidence`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /saɪt/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `cite` (/saɪt/ - trích dẫn)
  - **Noun**: `citation` (/saɪˈteɪ.ʃən/ - sự trích dẫn / nguồn trích dẫn)
  - **Adjective**: `cited` (/ˈsaɪ.tɪd/ - được trích dẫn)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `source citation` (trích dẫn nguồn)
  - `cite evidence` (trích dẫn bằng chứng)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Always **cite** official tech blogs when presenting financial data._
  - _Noun: Proper **citation** improves the academic credibility of your presentation._
  - _Adjective: The **cited** figures reflect Netflix's 2025/2026 earnings reports._
