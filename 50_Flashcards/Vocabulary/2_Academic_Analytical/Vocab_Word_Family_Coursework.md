What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **coursework**?

---

- **Meaning**: Work done during a course of study, usually assessed as part of a qualification (các học phần, môn học được đào tạo).
- **Primary Collocation**: `relevant coursework` / `core coursework`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈkɔːs.wɜːk/
- Type: Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `coursework` (/ˈkɔːs.wɜːk/ - môn học/học phần), `course` (/kɔːs/ - khóa học)
  - **Adjective**: `N/A`
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `relevant coursework` (các môn học liên quan)
  - `core coursework` (các môn học nòng cốt)
- Examples:
  - _Noun: Completed core **coursework** in Object-Oriented Programming and Database Systems._
  - _Noun: The computer science **coursework** provided practical systems experience._
