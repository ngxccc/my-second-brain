What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **engineer**?

---

- **Meaning**: To design, build, or construct a technical solution or pipeline with rigor (kỹ nghệ hóa, xây dựng giải pháp kỹ thuật).
- **Primary Collocation**: `engineer a pipeline` / `software engineering`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌen.dʒɪˈnɪər/
- Type: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `engineer` (/ˌen.dʒɪˈnɪər/ - kỹ nghệ hóa, thiết kế kỹ thuật)
  - **Noun**: `engineer` (/ˌen.dʒɪˈnɪər/ - kỹ sư), `engineering` (/ˌen.dʒɪˈnɪə.rɪŋ/ - ngành kỹ nghệ)
  - **Adjective**: `engineered` (/ˌen.dʒɪˈnɪəd/ - được thiết kế kỹ thuật)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `engineer a pipeline` (thiết kế đường ống kỹ thuật)
  - `software engineering` (kỹ nghệ phần mềm)
  - `reverse engineer` (dịch ngược kỹ thuật)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: We **engineered** a zero-downtime Blue-Green deployment pipeline._
  - _Noun: Strong **engineering** discipline ensures high system reliability._
