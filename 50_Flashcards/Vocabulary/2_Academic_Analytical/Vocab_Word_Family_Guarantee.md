What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **guarantee**?

---

- **Meaning**: To promise that something will happen, or to make something certain to happen (bảo đảm, cam kết).
- **Primary Collocation**: `guarantee data consistency` / `delivery guarantee`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌɡær.ənˈtiː/
- Type: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `guarantee` (/ˌɡær.ənˈtiː/ - bảo đảm)
  - **Noun**: `guarantee` (/ˌɡær.ənˈtiː/ - sự bảo đảm, cam kết)
  - **Adjective**: `guaranteed` (/ˌɡær.ənˈtiːd/ - được bảo đảm)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `guarantee data consistency` (bảo đảm tính nhất quán dữ liệu)
  - `delivery guarantee` (cam kết phân phát tin nhắn/dữ liệu)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: ACID transactions **guaranteed** data consistency during high-load checkouts._
  - _Noun: The outbox pattern provided a delivery **guarantee** for transactional emails._
