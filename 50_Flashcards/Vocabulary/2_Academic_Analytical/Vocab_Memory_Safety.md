What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **memory safety**?

---

- **Meaning**: A property of programming languages that protects against security bugs and undefined behaviors such as buffer overflows and use-after-free (tính an toàn bộ nhớ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `guarantee memory safety` / `memory safety vulnerabilities`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈmem.ər.i ˌseɪf.ti/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `guarantee memory safety` (đảm bảo an toàn bộ nhớ)
  - `memory safety vulnerabilities` (lỗ hổng an toàn bộ nhớ)
  - `memory safety at compile time` (an toàn bộ nhớ tại thời điểm biên dịch)
- Examples:
  - _Rust achieves **memory safety** through its borrow checker without the runtime overhead of garbage collection._
  - _Go provides **memory safety** by performing automatic bounds checking on slice accesses._
