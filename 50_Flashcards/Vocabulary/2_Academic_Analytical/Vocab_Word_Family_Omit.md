What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **omit**?

---

- **Meaning**: To leave out or exclude someone or something (bỏ qua, loại bỏ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `omit an image` / `intentional omission`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /əˈmɪt/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `omit` (/əˈmɪt/ - bỏ qua, loại bỏ)
  - **Noun**: `omission` (/əˈmɪʃ.ən/ - sự bỏ sót / sự loại bỏ)
  - **Adjective**: `omissible` (/əˈmɪs.ə.bəl/ - có thể bỏ qua)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `omit an image` (bỏ qua một hình ảnh)
  - `intentional omission` (sự loại bỏ có chủ đích)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: You can **omit** the mobile screenshot to keep the slide clean._
  - _Noun: The **omission** of the secondary image prevents visual clutter._
  - _Adjective: The mobile image is **omissible** since the TV UI illustrates the main concept._
