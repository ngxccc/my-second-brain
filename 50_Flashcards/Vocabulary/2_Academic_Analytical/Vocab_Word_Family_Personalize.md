What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **personalize**?

---

- **Meaning**: To design or alter something to meet an individual's specifications or preferences (cá nhân hóa).
- **Primary Collocation**: `personalized recommendations` / `artwork personalization`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈpɜː.sən.əl.aɪz/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `personalize` (/ˈpɜː.sən.əl.aɪz/ - cá nhân hóa)
  - **Noun**: `personalization` (/ˌpɜː.sən.əl.aɪˈzeɪ.ʃən/ - sự cá nhân hóa)
  - **Adjective**: `personalized` (/ˈpɜː.sən.əl.aɪzd/ - được cá nhân hóa)
  - **Adverb**: `personally` (/ˈpɜː.sən.əl.i/ - về mặt cá nhân / đích thân)
- Collocations:
  - `personalized recommendations` (gợi ý được cá nhân hóa)
  - `artwork personalization` (cá nhân hóa ảnh đại diện)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Netflix algorithms **personalize** thumbnails for over 260 million members._
  - _Noun: Artwork **personalization** improved user click-through rates by 20%._
  - _Adjective: The user receives a **personalized** homepage recommendation list._
  - _Adverb: The system adapts **personally** to each profile's viewing habits._
