What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **time allocation**?

---

- **Meaning**: The deliberate distribution and scheduling of available time across distinct tasks or priorities (sự phân bổ thời gian).
- **Primary Collocation**: `optimal time allocation` / `time allocation strategy`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈtaɪm ˌæl.əˈkeɪ.ʃən/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `optimal time allocation` (sự phân bổ thời gian tối ưu)
  - `time allocation strategy` (chiến lược phân bổ thời gian)
  - `manage time allocation` (quản lý việc phân bổ thời gian)
- Examples:
  - _Proper **time allocation** between system design and live coding ensures you finish the interview comfortably._
  - _His daily **time allocation** reserved 30 minutes for Anki spaced repetition review._
