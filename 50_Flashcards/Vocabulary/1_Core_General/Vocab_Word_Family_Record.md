What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **record**?

---

- **Meaning**: To store, document, or register information; a written or digital document of facts (ghi lại, lưu trữ hồ sơ; bản ghi).
- **Primary Collocation**: `record data / vocabulary` / `keep a record of`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: - Verb: /rɪˈkɔːd/ (nhấn âm 2)
- Type: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `record` (/rɪˈkɔːd/ - ghi lại, lưu trữ)
  - **Noun**: `record` (/ˈrek.ɔːd/ - bản ghi, hồ sơ) / `recording` (/rɪˈkɔː.dɪŋ/ - đoạn băng ghi âm/hình)
  - **Adjective**: `recorded` (/rɪˈkɔː.dɪd/ - đã được ghi lại) / `record-breaking` (/ˈrek.ɔːdˌbreɪ.kɪŋ/ - phá kỷ lục)
  - **Adverb**: `record-wise` (/ˈrek.ɔːd.waɪz/ - xét về mặt hồ sơ / kỷ lục)
- Collocations:
  - `record data / vocabulary` (ghi lại dữ liệu / từ vựng)
  - `keep a record of` (lưu trữ hồ sơ về cái gì)
  - `historical record` (bản ghi lịch sử)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The tutoring engine will **record** every unfamiliar technical chunk._
  - _Noun: The agent maintains an active **record** of learner errors in JSON._
  - _Adjective: The **recorded** error patterns help generate targeted review quizzes._
  - _Noun: High database throughput set a new **record** for transaction speed._
