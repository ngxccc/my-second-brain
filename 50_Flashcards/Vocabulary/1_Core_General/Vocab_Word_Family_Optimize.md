What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **optimize**?

---

- **Meaning**: To make something as effective, efficient, or functional as possible (tối ưu hóa).
- **Primary Collocation**: `optimize performance` / `optimal solution`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈɒp.tɪ.maɪz/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `optimize` (/ˈɒp.tɪ.maɪz/ - tối ưu hóa)
  - **Noun**: `optimization` (/ˌɒp.tɪ.maɪˈzeɪ.ʃən/ - sự tối ưu hóa)
  - **Adjective**: `optimal` (/ˈɒp.tɪ.məl/ - tối ưu)
  - **Adverb**: `optimally` (/ˈɒp.tɪ.mə.li/ - một cách tối ưu)
- Collocations:
  - `optimize performance` (tối ưu hóa hiệu năng)
  - `optimal solution` (giải pháp tối ưu)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: We need to **optimize** database query execution time._
  - _Noun: Memory **optimization** improved application throughput._
  - _Adjective: Indexing provides an **optimal** search experience._
  - _Adverb: The system functions **optimally** under high load._
