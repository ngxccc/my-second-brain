What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **whiteboard**?

---

- **Meaning**: A smooth, glossy surface for non-permanent markings (bảng trắng)
- **Primary Collocation**: `whiteboard session` / `whiteboard an architecture`

---

Extra:

  - **Adjective**: `N/A`
