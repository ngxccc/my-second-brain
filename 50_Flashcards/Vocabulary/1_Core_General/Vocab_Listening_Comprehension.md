What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **listening comprehension**?

---

- **Meaning**: The cognitive ability to understand, process, and interpret spoken language (kỹ năng nghe hiểu).
- **Primary Collocation**: `develop listening comprehension` / `listening comprehension exercises`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈlɪs.ən.ɪŋ ˌkɑːm.prəˈhen.ʃən/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `develop listening comprehension` (phát triển kỹ năng nghe hiểu)
  - `listening comprehension exercises` (các bài tập luyện nghe hiểu)
  - `sharp listening comprehension` (khả năng nghe hiểu nhạy bén)
- Examples:
  - _Dictation practice with technical podcasts sharpens your **listening comprehension**._
  - _Good **listening comprehension** is essential for understanding ambiguous system requirements from stakeholders._
