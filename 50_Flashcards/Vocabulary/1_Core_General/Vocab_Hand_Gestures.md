What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **hand gestures**?

---

- **Meaning**: Movements of the hands used to express ideas, emphasize points, or communicate without speaking (cử chỉ tay).
- **Primary Collocation**: `natural hand gestures` / `expressive hand gestures`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈhænd ˌdʒes.tʃɚz/
- Type: Noun Phrase / Collocation
- Collocations:
  - `natural hand gestures` (cử chỉ tay tự nhiên)
  - `expressive hand gestures` (cử chỉ tay giàu tính biểu cảm)
  - `open box zone gestures` (cử chỉ tay trong vùng hộp mở khi thuyết trình)
- Examples:
  - _Using natural **hand gestures** keeps the audience engaged during an architecture whiteboard presentation._
  - _Clear **hand gestures** emphasize transitions between system components._
