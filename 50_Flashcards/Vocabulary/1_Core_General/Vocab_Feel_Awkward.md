It is completely normal to ~~feel awkward~~ when you start presenting technical concepts in English for the first time.

---

Extra:

- Meaning: To experience discomfort, embarrassment, or self-consciousness in an unfamiliar situation (cảm thấy ngượng ngùng, lúng túng).
- Pronunciation: /fiːl ˈɔː.kwɚd/
- Collocations:
  - `feel awkward about something` (cảm thấy ngượng ngùng về điều gì)
  - `make someone feel awkward` (khiến ai đó cảm thấy lúng túng)
  - `overcome feeling awkward` (vượt qua cảm giác ngượng ngùng)
