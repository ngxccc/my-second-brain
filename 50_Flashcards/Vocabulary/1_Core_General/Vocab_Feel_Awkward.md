What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **feel awkward**?

---

- **Meaning**: To experience discomfort, embarrassment, or self-consciousness in an unfamiliar situation (cảm thấy ngượng ngùng, lúng túng).
- **Primary Collocation**: `feel awkward about something` / `make someone feel awkward`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /fiːl ˈɔː.kwɚd/
- Type: Verb Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `feel awkward about something` (cảm thấy ngượng ngùng về điều gì)
  - `make someone feel awkward` (khiến ai đó cảm thấy lúng túng)
  - `overcome feeling awkward` (vượt qua cảm giác ngượng ngùng)
- Examples:
  - _It is completely normal to **feel awkward** when you start presenting technical concepts in English for the first time._
  - _Repeated whiteboard rehearsals eliminate the tendency to **feel awkward** during live screens._
