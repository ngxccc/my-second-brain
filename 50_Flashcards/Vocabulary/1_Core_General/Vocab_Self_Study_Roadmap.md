What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **self study roadmap**?

---

- **Meaning**: A structured, self-directed learning plan detailing milestones, resources, and timelines to achieve a target competency (lộ trình tự học).
- **Primary Collocation**: `build a self study roadmap` / `follow a self study roadmap`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌself ˈstʌd.i ˈroʊd.mæp/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `build a self study roadmap` (xây dựng lộ trình tự học)
  - `follow a self study roadmap` (bám sát lộ trình tự học)
  - `technical self study roadmap` (lộ trình tự học kỹ thuật)
- Examples:
  - _His **self study roadmap** focused on Go concurrency and Kubernetes internals._
  - _A clear **self study roadmap** prevents decision fatigue and keeps daily practice consistent._
