What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **manage**?

---

- **Meaning**: To control, direct, or handle tasks, resources, or systems (quản lý).
- **Primary Collocation**: `manage resources` / `memory management`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈmæn.ɪdʒ/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `manage` (/ˈmæn.ɪdʒ/ - quản lý)
  - **Noun**: `management` (/ˈmæn.ɪdʒ.mənt/ - sự quản lý) / `manager` (/ˈmæn.ɪdʒ.ər/ - người quản lý)
  - **Adjective**: `manageable` (/ˈmæn.ɪdʒ.ə.bəl/ - có thể quản lý được)
  - **Adverb**: `manageably` (/ˈmæn.ɪdʒ.ə.bli/ - một cách quản lý được)
- Collocations:
  - `manage resources` (quản lý tài nguyên)
  - `memory management` (quản lý bộ nhớ)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The engine **manages** memory allocations efficiently._
  - _Noun: Proper memory **management** prevents leaks._
  - _Adjective: The project scope remains **manageable** with sprints._
