What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **out loud**?

---

- **Meaning**: With a normal voice so that other people can hear; not silently or in one's head (thành tiếng, to rõ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `read out loud` / `speak out loud`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌaʊt ˈlaʊd/
- Type: Adverb Chunk
- Collocations:
  - `read out loud` (đọc to thành tiếng)
  - `speak out loud` (nói to ra thành tiếng)
  - `think out loud` (nói ra suy nghĩ của mình thành lời)
- Examples:
  - _Thinking **out loud** helps the interviewer follow your thought process during a technical screen._
  - _You don't need to speak **out loud** when practicing late at night; whispering activates the same motor cortex._
