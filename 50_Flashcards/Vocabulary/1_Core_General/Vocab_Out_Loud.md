Thinking ~~out loud~~ helps the interviewer follow your thought process during a technical screen.

---

Extra:

- Meaning: With a normal voice so that other people can hear; not silently or in one's head (thành tiếng, to rõ).
- Pronunciation: /ˌaʊt ˈlaʊd/
- Collocations:
  - `read out loud` (đọc to thành tiếng)
  - `speak out loud` (nói to ra thành tiếng)
  - `think out loud` (nói ra suy nghĩ của mình thành lời)
