What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **respond**?

---

- **Meaning**: To answer, react, or take responsibility for an action or request (phản hồi, chịu trách nhiệm).
- **Primary Collocation**: `be responsible for` / `rapid response`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /rɪˈspɒnd/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `respond` (/rɪˈspɒnd/ - phản hồi)
  - **Noun**: `response` (/rɪˈspɒns/ - sự phản hồi) / `responsibility` (/rɪˌspɒn.səˈbɪl.ə.ti/ - trách nhiệm)
  - **Adjective**: `responsible` (/rɪˈspɒn.sə.bəl/ - chịu trách nhiệm)
  - **Adverb**: `responsibly` (/rɪˈspɒn.sə.bli/ - một cách có trách nhiệm)
- Collocations:
  - `be responsible for` (chịu trách nhiệm cho việc gì)
  - `rapid response` (phản hồi nhanh chóng)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The API **responds** within 50 milliseconds._
  - _Noun: Engineers have a **responsibility** to write secure code._
  - _Adjective: The auth service is **responsible** for handling user login tokens._
  - _Adverb: Engineers handle production incidents **responsibly**._
