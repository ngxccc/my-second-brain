What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **opportunity**?

---

- **Meaning**: A set of circumstances that makes it possible to do something (cơ hội, thời cơ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `seize an opportunity` / `career opportunities`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: - Singular: `/ˌɑː.pɚˈtuː.nə.t̬i/` (Syllable breakdown: `op-por-TU-ni-ty`)
- Type: Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `opportunity` (/ˌɑː.pɚˈtuː.nə.t̬i/ - cơ hội), `opportunities` (/ˌɑː.pɚˈtuː.nə.t̬iz/ - các cơ hội)
  - **Adjective**: `opportune` (/ˌɑː.pɚˈtuːn/ - đúng lúc, kịp thời), `opportunistic` (/ˌɑː.pɚ.təˈnɪs.tɪk/ - có tính chớp thời cơ)
  - **Adverb**: `opportunely` (/ˌɑː.pɚˈtuːn.li/ - một cách đúng lúc)
- Collocations:
  - `seize an opportunity` (nắm bắt cơ hội)
  - `career opportunities` (cơ hội nghề nghiệp)
  - `weigh opportunities and risks` (cân nhắc cơ hội và rủi ro)
- Examples:
  - _Noun: Weighing all these **opportunities** and risks helps us find the optimal roadmap._
  - _Adjective: It was an **opportune** moment to integrate automated testing tools._
