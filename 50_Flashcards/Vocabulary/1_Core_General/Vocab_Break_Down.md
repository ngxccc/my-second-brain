What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **break down**?

---

- **Meaning**: To separate a complex system, concept, or problem into smaller, simpler, more manageable parts (phân tách, bóc tách, chia nhỏ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `break down a problem` / `break down into components`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌbreɪk ˈdaʊn/
- Type: Phrasal Verb
- Collocations:
  - `break down a problem` (bóc tách một vấn đề phức tạp)
  - `break down into components` (chia nhỏ thành các thành phần)
  - `break down the architecture` (phân tích chi tiết kiến trúc)
- Examples:
  - _Always **break down** high-level system requirements into atomic sub-tasks before implementing._
  - _The engineer **broke down** the monolithic service into decoupled background workers._
