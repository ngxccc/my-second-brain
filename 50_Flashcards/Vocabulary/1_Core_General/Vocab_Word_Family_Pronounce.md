What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **pronounce**?

---

- **Meaning**: To make the sound of a word or letter in a specific way (phát âm).
- **Primary Collocation**: `correct pronunciation` / `pronounce words`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /prəˈnaʊns/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `pronounce` (/prəˈnaʊns/ - phát âm)
  - **Noun**: `pronunciation` (/prəˌnʌn.siˈeɪ.ʃən/ - sự phát âm)
  - **Adjective**: `pronounceable` (/prəˈnaʊn.sə.bəl/ - có thể phát âm)
  - **Adverb**: `pronouncingly` (/prəˈnaʊn.sɪŋ.li/ - một cách dứt khoát)
- Collocations:
  - `correct pronunciation` (sự phát âm chính xác)
  - `pronounce words` (phát âm từ ngữ)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: How do you **pronounce** this technical term?_
  - _Noun: What is the correct **pronunciation** of this word?_
  - _Adjective: Ensure variable names are easily **pronounceable** in code reviews._
