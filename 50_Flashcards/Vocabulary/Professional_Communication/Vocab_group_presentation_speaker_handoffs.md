What are the core professional patterns and phrases for **Speaker Handoffs** (Passing the Mic) in group presentations?

---

- **Core Concept (Speaker Handoff - Chuyển giao người nói)**:
  - Trong thuyết trình nhóm, sự chuyển giao mượt mà giữa các thành viên tạo ấn tượng chuyên nghiệp cao, tránh sự im lặng gượng gạo (_awkward silence_).
- **Pattern 1: Outgoing Speaker (Người chuẩn bị nhường mic)**:
  - _"Now, I'll hand over / pass the mic to [Name] to walk you through [Topic]."_
  - _"Next, [Name] will cover [Topic] on the upcoming slide."_
- **Pattern 2: Incoming Speaker Acceptance Lead-in (Người vừa nhận mic)**:
  - _"Thank you, [Name]. [PAUSE] So, what happens if...?"_
  - _"Thanks, [Name]. [PAUSE] Let's dive into..."_
- **Pattern 3: The Closer / Wrap-up (Người chốt bài kết luận)**:
  - _"To bring everything together, our conclusion is crystal clear..."_
  - _"Finally, let's look at our final verdict and vision..."_
- **Concrete Example**:
  - Outgoing: _"Now, I'll pass the mic to Ngoc to discuss the risks of total AI dependency."_
  - Incoming (Closer): _"Thank you, Tai. So, what happens if a company relies 100% on AI without human engineers?"_
