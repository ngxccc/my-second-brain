What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **off the clock**?

---

- **Type**: Idiom / Prepositional Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌɔːf ðə ˈklɑːk/
- **Meaning**: Outside of official working hours; not being paid or scheduled to work (ngoài giờ làm việc, thời gian riêng tư).
- **Collocations**:
  - `work off the clock` (làm việc ngoài giờ không lương)
  - `discuss topics off the clock` (thảo luận sau giờ làm)
  - `strictly off the clock` (hoàn toàn ngoài giờ làm việc)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Engineers should not be pressured to answer non-critical Slack alerts when they are **off the clock**._
  - _I enjoy building side projects in Rust **off the clock** to expand my systems knowledge._
