What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **multiple choice question**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌmʌl.tə.pəl ˈtʃɔɪs ˌkwes.tʃən/
- **Meaning**: An objective assessment question where test-takers choose the best answer from a set of provided alternatives (câu hỏi trắc nghiệm nhiều lựa chọn).
- **Collocations**:
  - `standard multiple choice question` (câu hỏi trắc nghiệm tiêu chuẩn)
  - `answer a multiple choice question` (trả lời câu hỏi trắc nghiệm)
  - `multiple choice question format` (dạng câu hỏi trắc nghiệm)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _TOEIC Reading Part 5 consists of 30 **multiple choice questions** testing grammar and vocabulary._
  - _Unlike coding drills, a **multiple choice question** tests recognition rather than active production._
