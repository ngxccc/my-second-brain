What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **advance rapidly**?

---

- **Type**: Verb Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ədˈvæns ˈræp.ɪd.li/
- **Meaning**: To make fast, steady progress or develop forward with high momentum (tiến bộ nhanh chóng, phát triển vượt bậc).
- **Collocations**:
  - `advance rapidly in one's career` (thăng tiến nhanh chóng trong sự nghiệp)
  - `advance rapidly through milestones` (tiến nhanh qua các cột mốc)
  - `technology advances rapidly` (công nghệ phát triển vượt bậc)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Engineers who master first principles **advance rapidly** in system architecture competency._
  - _Continuous spaced repetition helped his technical vocabulary **advance rapidly**._
