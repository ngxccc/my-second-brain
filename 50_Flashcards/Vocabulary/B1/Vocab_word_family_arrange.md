What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **arrange**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /əˈreɪndʒ/
- **Meaning**: To plan, prepare, or put in order ahead of time; to organize an event or meeting (sắp xếp, lên kế hoạch, thu xếp).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `arrange` (/əˈreɪndʒ/ - sắp xếp, thu xếp) / `rearrange` (/ˌriː.əˈreɪndʒ/ - sắp xếp lại)
  - **Noun**: `arrangement` (/əˈreɪndʒ.mənt/ - sự sắp đặt, kế hoạch đã chuẩn bị) / `rearrangement` (/ˌriː.əˈreɪndʒ.mənt/ - sự sắp xếp lại)
  - **Adjective**: `arranged` (/əˈreɪndʒd/ - đã được sắp xếp)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `make arrangements for something` (chuẩn bị, thu xếp cho việc gì)
  - `fixed future arrangements` (các kế hoạch sắp xếp cố định trong tương lai)
  - `arrange a technical interview` (sắp xếp một buổi phỏng vấn kỹ thuật)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: The team **arranged** a live whiteboard architecture review for next Tuesday._
  - _Noun: We have already made travel **arrangements** and booked flights for the Paris conference._
