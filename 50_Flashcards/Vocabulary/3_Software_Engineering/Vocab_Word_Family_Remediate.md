What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **remediate**?

---

- **Meaning**: To correct, fix, or provide a remedy for a flaw, vulnerability, or design issue (khắc phục, xử lý dứt điểm khiếm khuyết/lỗi).
- **Primary Collocation**: `remediate vulnerabilities / leaks` / `remediation plan`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /rɪˈmiː.di.eɪt/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `remediate` (/rɪˈmiː.di.eɪt/ - khắc phục, sửa chữa lỗi/lỗ hổng)
  - **Noun**: `remediation` (/rɪˌmiː.diˈeɪ.ʃən/ - sự khắc phục, quá trình xử lý lỗi)
  - **Adjective**: `remediable` (/rɪˈmiː.di.ə.bəl/ - có thể khắc phục được) / `remedial` (/rɪˈmiː.di.əl/ - mang tính khắc phục/bổ cứu)
  - **Adverb**: `remedially` (/rɪˈmiː.di.əl.i/ - một cách bổ cứu/khắc phục)
- Collocations:
  - `remediate vulnerabilities / leaks` (khắc phục các lỗ hổng / rò rỉ)
  - `remediation plan` (kế hoạch khắc phục sự cố)
  - `prompt remediation` (sự khắc phục kịp thời)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The team audited the vault to **remediate** leaked answers across all flashcards._
  - _Noun: Automated security scanning accelerates vulnerability **remediation**._
  - _Adjective: The cognitive bias was easily **remediable** through active recall training._
  - _Adjective: The mentor provided **remedial** grammar exercises to fix recurring mistakes._
