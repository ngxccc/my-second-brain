What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **compress**?

---

- **Meaning**: To flatten or squeeze together; in computer science, to reduce data size using encoding algorithms (nén dữ liệu, ép lại).
- **Primary Collocation**: `gzip / brotli compression` / `compressed payload`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /kəmˈpres/ (Verb) | /ˈkɑːm.pres/ (Noun)
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `compress` (/kəmˈpres/ - nén dữ liệu)
  - **Noun**: `compression` (/kəmˈpreʃ.ən/ - sự nén, chuẩn nén)
  - **Adjective**: `compressed` (/kəmˈprest/ - đã được nén, ở trạng thái nén) / `compressible` (/kəmˈpres.ə.bəl/ - có thể nén được)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `gzip / brotli compression` (thuật toán nén Gzip / Brotli)
  - `compressed payload` (dữ liệu payload đã được nén)
  - `lossless compression` (nén không mất dữ liệu)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The reverse proxy **compresses** HTTP response bodies with Brotli._
  - _Noun: Data **compression** significantly reduces network bandwidth usage._
  - _Adjective: Is the JSON payload **compressed** before transmission?_
