What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **orchestra**?

---

- **Meaning**: - Literal: A large ensemble of musical instruments playing classical or symphonic works (dàn nhạc giao hưởng).
  - Metaphorical/Tech: An organized and coordinat...
- **Primary Collocation**: `an orchestra of AI agents` / `orchestrate a workflow`
