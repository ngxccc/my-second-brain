What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **contiguous memory**?

---

- **Meaning**: A single unbroken, continuous block of address space in memory with no intervening gaps (khối bộ nhớ liên tục).
- **Primary Collocation**: `store in contiguous memory` / `allocate contiguous memory`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /kənˈtɪɡ.ju.əs ˈmem.ər.i/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `store in contiguous memory` (lưu trữ trong khối bộ nhớ liên tục)
  - `allocate contiguous memory` (cấp phát bộ nhớ liền kề)
  - `scan contiguous memory` (quét tuần tự qua vùng nhớ liên tục)
- Examples:
  - _Hardware prefetchers can predict access patterns easily when data resides in **contiguous memory**._
  - _Arrays occupy **contiguous memory**, whereas node-based trees spread pointers across the heap._
