What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **integrate**?

---

- **Meaning**: To combine two or more things in order to become more effective (tích hợp hệ thống, dịch vụ).
- **Primary Collocation**: `integrate payment gateway` / `continuous integration`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈɪn.tɪ.ɡreɪt/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `integrate` (/ˈɪn.tɪ.ɡreɪt/ - tích hợp)
  - **Noun**: `integration` (/ˌɪn.tɪˈɡreɪ.ʃən/ - sự tích hợp), `integrity` (/ɪnˈteɡ.rə.ti/ - tính toàn vẹn)
  - **Adjective**: `integrated` (/ˈɪn.tɪ.ɡreɪ.tɪd/ - được tích hợp)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `integrate payment gateway` (tích hợp cổng thanh toán)
  - `continuous integration` (tích hợp liên tục - CI)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The backend **integrated** PayOS payment gateway with webhook verification._
  - _Noun: Seamless third-party **integration** simplified the billing flow._
