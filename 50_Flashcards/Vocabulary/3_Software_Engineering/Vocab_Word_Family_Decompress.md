What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **decompress**?

---

- **Meaning**: To relieve pressure, or to relax and unwind after a stressful period (xả áp lực, giải tỏa căng thẳng).
- **Primary Collocation**: `decompress after a long day` / `a decompression routine`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌdiː.kəmˈpres/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `decompress` (/ˌdiː.kəmˈpres/ - xả áp lực)
  - **Noun**: `decompression` (/ˌdiː.kəmˈpreʃ.ən/ - sự xả áp lực)
  - **Adjective**: `decompressed` (/ˌdiː.kəmˈprest/)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `decompress after a long day` (xả áp lực sau ngày dài)
  - `a decompression routine` (thói quen xả căng thẳng)
  - `mental decompression` (xả nạp nhận thức)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Taking a 15-minute walk without a phone helps your brain **decompress**._
  - _Noun: Non-sleep deep rest provides instant mental **decompression**._
  - _Adjective: I felt completely **decompressed** after the weekend trip._
