What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **slice literal**?

---

- **Meaning**: A concise syntax construct used to declare and initialize a slice directly by specifying elements inside curly braces `{}` (cú pháp khởi tạo slice trực tiếp).
- **Primary Collocation**: `initialize with a slice literal` / `slice literal syntax`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈslaɪs ˌlɪt̬.ɚ.əl/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `initialize with a slice literal`
  - `slice literal syntax`
  - `empty slice literal `[]T{}``
- Examples:
  - _Using a **slice literal** like `[]string{"go", "rust"}` implicitly constructs a backing array and returns a slice header._
