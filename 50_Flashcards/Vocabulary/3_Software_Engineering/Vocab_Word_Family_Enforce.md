What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **enforce**?

---

- **Meaning**: To make people or systems obey a rule, constraint, or schema (bắt buộc tuân thủ, áp đặt quy tắc).
- **Primary Collocation**: `enforce type safety` / `enforce validation rules`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ɪnˈfɔːs/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `enforce` (/ɪnˈfɔːs/ - bắt buộc tuân thủ)
  - **Noun**: `enforcement` (/ɪnˈfɔːs.mənt/ - sự thực thi bắt buộc)
  - **Adjective**: `enforceable` (/ɪnˈfɔː.sə.bəl/ - có thể bắt buộc thi hành)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- Collocations:
  - `enforce type safety` (bắt buộc an toàn kiểu dữ liệu)
  - `enforce validation rules` (bắt buộc tuân thủ quy tắc xác thực)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: The Turborepo monorepo **enforced** compile-time type validation across apps._
  - _Noun: Strict schema **enforcement** prevented malformed database payloads._
