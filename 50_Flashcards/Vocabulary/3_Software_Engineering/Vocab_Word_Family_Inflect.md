What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **inflect**?

---

- **Meaning**: In grammar, to change the form of a word (by adding prefixes, suffixes, or vowel shifts) to express tense, gender, number, or case
- **Primary Collocation**: `inflectional morphology` / `inflected language`

---

Extra:
