What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **side effect**?

---

- **Meaning**: A secondary, typically undesirable consequence
- **Primary Collocation**: `pure functions without side effects` / `unexpected side effects`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈsaɪd ɪˌfekt/
- Type: Noun Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `pure functions without side effects` (hàm thuần khiết không có tác dụng phụ)
  - `unexpected side effects` (tác dụng phụ không lường trước)
  - `isolate side effects in database layers` (cô lập tác dụng phụ ở tầng dữ liệu)
- Examples:
  - _Mutating a global variable inside a helper function introduces dangerous hidden **side effects**._
  - _Pure functions make unit testing effortless because they produce no **side effects**._
