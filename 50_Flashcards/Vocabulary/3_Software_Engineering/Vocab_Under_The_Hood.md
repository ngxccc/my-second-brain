What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **under the hood**?

---

- **Meaning**: Referring to the underlying, internal mechanisms, implementation details, or workings of a system that are hidden beneath an interface (bên dưới cơ chế nội tại ngầm).
- **Primary Collocation**: `how it works under the hood` / `look under the hood`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌʌn.dɚ ðə ˈhʊd/
- Type: Idiomatic Prepositional Phrase
- Collocations:
  - `how it works under the hood` (cách nó vận hành dưới nắp ca-pô)
  - `look under the hood` (soi kỹ cơ chế bên dưới)
  - `understand runtime mechanics under the hood` (hiểu cơ chế runtime ẩn sâu bên dưới)
- Examples:
  - _While slices feel like dynamic arrays, **under the hood** they are backed by fixed-size contiguous memory blocks._
  - _Senior engineers know what happens **under the hood** when a goroutine switches execution contexts._
