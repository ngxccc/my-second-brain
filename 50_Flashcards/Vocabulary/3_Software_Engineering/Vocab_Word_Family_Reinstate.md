What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **reinstate**?

---

- **Meaning**: To restore someone or something to their former position, state, or availability
- **Primary Collocation**: `reinstate a feature / service` / `request reinstatement`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˌriː.ɪnˈsteɪt/
- Type: Verb (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `reinstate` (/ˌriː.ɪnˈsteɪt/ - khôi phục lại, mở lại)
  - **Noun**: `reinstatement` (/ˌriː.ɪnˈsteɪt.mənt/ - sự khôi phục lại, sự phục hồi)
  - **Adjective**: `reinstated` (/ˌriː.ɪnˈsteɪ.tɪd/ - đã được khôi phục)
- Collocations:
  - `reinstate a feature / service` (khôi phục lại một tính năng / dịch vụ)
  - `request reinstatement` (yêu cầu mở lại, khôi phục lại)
  - `reinstate access` (cấp lại quyền truy cập)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Users sent feedback requesting the developer to **reinstate** the YouTube shadowing tool._
  - _Noun: The team considered the **reinstatement** of custom video imports after high user demand._
  - _Adjective: The **reinstated** feature quickly became the most used tool on the platform._
