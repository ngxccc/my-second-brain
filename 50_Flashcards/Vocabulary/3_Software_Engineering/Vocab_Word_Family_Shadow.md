What is the core meaning and primary technical collocation of **shadow**?

---

- **Meaning**: To follow closely; in language learning, a technique where you listen to native speech and repeat it immediately with minimal delay (nghe nhại, kỹ thuật shadowi...
- **Primary Collocation**: `shadowing technique` / `job shadowing`

---

Extra:
- Pronunciation: /ˈʃæd.oʊ/
- Type: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- Word Family:
  - **Verb**: `shadow` (/ˈʃæd.oʊ/ - nghe nhại theo, đi theo quan sát/học việc)
  - **Noun**: `shadow` (/ˈʃæd.oʊ/ - cái bóng) / `shadowing` (/ˈʃæd.oʊ.ɪŋ/ - kỹ thuật nghe nhại, việc đi theo học hỏi)
  - **Adjective**: `shadowy` (/ˈʃæd.oʊ.i/ - mờ ảo, thuộc về bóng râm)
- Collocations:
  - `shadowing technique` (kỹ thuật luyện nghe nói shadowing)
  - `job shadowing` (hình thức đi theo quan sát thực tế công việc của chuyên gia)
  - `cast a shadow over` (phủ bóng đen lên điều gì)
- Examples:
  - _Verb: Language learners **shadow** native speakers on YouTube to master connected speech and intonation._
  - _Noun: Consistent **shadowing** practice drastically improves spoken rhythm and speech rate._
  - _Verb: The junior developer **shadowed** the lead architect during the migration review._
