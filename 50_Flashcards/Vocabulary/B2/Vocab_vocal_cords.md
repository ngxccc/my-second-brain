What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **vocal cords**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈvəʊ.kəl kɔːdz/
- **Meaning**: Two folds of tissue in the throat (larynx) that vibrate to produce voice and voiced sounds.
- **Example**:
  - _The sound /v/ in "I've" requires your **vocal cords** to vibrate._
