What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **fundamental question**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌfʌn.dəˈmen.t̬əl ˈkwes.tʃən/
- **Meaning**: A core, primary question that cuts to the root essence, first principles, or foundational mechanics of a problem (câu hỏi nền tảng, câu hỏi cốt lõi).
- **Collocations**:
  - `ask the fundamental question` (đặt câu hỏi nền tảng)
  - `address a fundamental question` (giải quyết một câu hỏi cốt lõi)
  - `the fundamental question of system design` (câu hỏi cốt lõi trong thiết kế hệ thống)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _The **fundamental question** in distributed storage is whether to prioritize strong consistency or high availability._
  - _First-principles thinking begins by asking the most **fundamental question** about the system's operational goal._
