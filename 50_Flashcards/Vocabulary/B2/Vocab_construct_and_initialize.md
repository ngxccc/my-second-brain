What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **construct and initialize**?

---

- **Type**: Verb Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /kənˈstrʌkt ænd ɪˈnɪʃ.əl.aɪz/
- **Meaning**: To allocate memory for an object and set its starting properties or state to valid defaults (xây dựng và khởi tạo giá trị ban đầu).
- **Collocations**:
  - `construct and initialize a struct`
  - `construct and initialize in one step`
  - `safely construct and initialize`
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Factory functions in Go **construct and initialize** services with proper configuration._
