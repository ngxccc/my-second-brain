What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **grasp**?

---

- **Type**: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ɡræsp/
- **Meaning**: To understand a concept completely and deeply (thấu hiểu sâu sắc); or to grip firmly with hands (nắm bắt chắc chắn).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `grasp` (/ɡræsp/ - thấu hiểu, nắm bắt)
  - **Noun**: `grasp` (/ɡræsp/ - sự thấu hiểu, khả năng nắm bắt)
  - **Adjective**: `graspable` (/ˈɡræs.pə.bəl/ - có thể hiểu được)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `grasp the core mechanics` (thấu hiểu cơ chế cốt lõi)
  - `a firm grasp of concurrency` (nắm vững kiến thức đồng thời)
  - `difficult to grasp` (khó nắm bắt)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Once you **grasp** pointer semantics, Go memory management becomes intuitive._
  - _Noun: Candidates with a firm **grasp** of networking fundamentals excel in backend interviews._
