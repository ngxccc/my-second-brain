What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **dictation method**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /dɪkˈteɪ.ʃən ˌmeθ.əd/
- **Meaning**: A language learning technique where the student listens to audio speech and transcribes every spoken word verbatim to train phonetics and ear precision (phương pháp chép chính tả).
- **Collocations**:
  - `apply the dictation method` (áp dụng phương pháp chép chính tả)
  - `dictation method for TOEIC Part 2` (phương pháp chép chính tả cho TOEIC Part 2)
  - `rigorous dictation method` (phương pháp chép chính tả nghiêm ngặt)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _The **dictation method** forces your brain to register subtle unstressed prepositions and weak forms._
  - _Practicing with the **dictation method** improved his listening comprehension score significantly._
