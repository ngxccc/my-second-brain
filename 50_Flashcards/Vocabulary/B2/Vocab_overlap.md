What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **overlap**?

---

- **Type**: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: Verb: /ˌoʊ.vɚˈlæp/ | Noun: /ˈoʊ.vɚ.læp/
- **Meaning**: To extend over and cover part of something else; to have elements or duties in common; in systems: memory regions or time intervals that intersect (chồng chéo, đè lên nhau).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `overlap` (/ˌoʊ.vɚˈlæp/ - chồng chéo lên nhau)
  - **Noun**: `overlap` (/ˈoʊ.vɚ.læp/ - sự chồng chéo, phần giao nhau)
  - **Adjective**: `overlapping` (/ˌoʊ.vɚˈlæp.ɪŋ/ - mang tính chồng chéo)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `overlapping memory regions` (các vùng nhớ chồng chéo lên nhau)
  - `avoid schedule overlap` (tránh chồng chéo lịch trình)
  - `significant overlap in functionality` (sự trùng lặp đáng kể về mặt chức năng)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Two slice views can **overlap** on the exact same underlying array in memory._
  - _Noun: Using `copy()` in Go safely handles **overlap** between destination and source memory slices._
