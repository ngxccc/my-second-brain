What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **feynman technique**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈfaɪn.mən tekˌniːk/
- **Meaning**: A mental model and learning framework where one learns a complex topic by teaching or explaining it in simple, jargon-free language to reveal cognitive gaps (kỹ thuật học Feynman).
- **Collocations**:
  - `apply the feynman technique` (áp dụng kỹ thuật Feynman)
  - `feynman technique whiteboard session` (buổi thuyết trình bảng trắng theo kỹ thuật Feynman)
  - `master concepts via the feynman technique` (làm chủ khái niệm qua kỹ thuật Feynman)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Using the **feynman technique**, he explained Go memory escape analysis using a simple restaurant kitchen metaphor._
  - _The **feynman technique** quickly exposes whether you truly understand a distributed algorithm or merely memorized syntax._
