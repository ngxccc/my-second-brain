What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **connected speech**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /kəˈnek.tɪd ˈspiːtʃ/
- **Meaning**: Spoken language in a continuous sequence where words link, merge, assimilate, or omit sounds compared to their isolated dictionary forms (ngữ lưu, chuỗi lời nói liên tục nối âm).
- **Collocations**:
  - `features of connected speech` (các đặc trưng của ngữ lưu nối âm)
  - `master connected speech` (làm chủ hiện tượng nối âm trong lời nói)
  - `connected speech phonetics` (ngữ âm học ngữ lưu)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Understanding **connected speech** like catenation and elision is essential for English listening comprehension._
  - _In **connected speech**, "would you" smoothly assimilates into `/ˈwʊdʒ.uː/`._
