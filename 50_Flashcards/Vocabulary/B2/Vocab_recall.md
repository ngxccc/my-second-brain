What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **recall**?

---

- **Type**: Verb / Noun (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /rɪˈkɑːl/
- **Meaning**: To retrieve information, facts, or concepts from long-term memory (nhớ lại, gợi lại ký ức); or to officially bring back or retract something (thu hồi).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `recall` (/rɪˈkɑːl/ - nhớ lại, thu hồi)
  - **Noun**: `recall` (/rɪˈkɑːl/ - khả năng hồi tưởng, lệnh thu hồi)
  - **Adjective**: `recallable` (/rɪˈkɑː.lə.bəl/ - có thể nhớ lại được)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `active recall practice` (thực hành chủ động gợi nhớ / active recall)
  - `effortful recall strengthens memory` (việc nỗ lực nhớ lại giúp củng cố trí nhớ)
  - `recall technical terms under pressure` (nhớ lại thuật ngữ kỹ thuật dưới áp lực)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Noun: **Active recall** via Anki flashcards produces stronger neural connections than passive re-reading._
  - _Verb: During the system design interview, he could easily **recall** the formula for Raft leader election._
