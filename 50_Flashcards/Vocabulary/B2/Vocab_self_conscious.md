What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **self conscious**?

---

- **Type**: Adjective (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌselfˈkɑːn.ʃəs/
- **Meaning**: Feeling uncomfortably aware of one's appearance, speech, actions, or perceived shortcomings in front of others (e dè, tự ti, ngượng ngùng về bản thân).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `N/A`
  - **Noun**: `self-consciousness` (/ˌselfˈkɑːn.ʃəs.nəs/ - sự e dè, tâm lý ngượng ngùng)
  - **Adjective**: `self-conscious` (/ˌselfˈkɑːn.ʃəs/ - e dè, tự ti)
  - **Adverb**: `self-consciously` (/ˌselfˈkɑːn.ʃəs.li/ - một cách e dè)
- **Collocations**:
  - `feel self-conscious speaking English` (cảm thấy tự ti khi nói tiếng Anh)
  - `overcome being self-conscious` (vượt qua tâm lý e dè)
  - `painfully self-conscious` (vô cùng ngượng ngùng)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Adjective: Beginners often feel **self-conscious** about pronunciation errors when speaking in meetings._
  - _Adverb: He paused **self-consciously** before delivering his first English whiteboard explanation._
