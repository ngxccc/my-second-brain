What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **regenerate**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /rɪˈdʒen.ə.reɪt/
- **Meaning**: To create, form, or build again; to restore to original vigor; in software: to rebuild generated code, tokens, or cache artifacts (tái sinh, phục hồi, sinh lại mã/dữ liệu).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `regenerate` (/rɪˈdʒen.ə.reɪt/ - tái tạo, sinh lại)
  - **Noun**: `regeneration` (/rɪˌdʒen.əˈreɪ.ʃən/ - sự tái sinh) / `regenerator` (/rɪˈdʒen.ə.reɪ.t̬ɚ/ - bộ tái tạo)
  - **Adjective**: `regenerative` (/rɪˈdʒen.ə.rə.t̬ɪv/ - có tính phục hồi)
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `regenerate protobuf code` (sinh lại mã nguồn Protocol Buffers)
  - `regenerate expired auth tokens` (sinh lại token xác thực đã hết hạn)
  - `regenerate the cache` (tạo lại bộ nhớ đệm)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: Run `go generate ./...` to **regenerate** the gRPC client stubs after updating the schema._
  - _Verb: The background worker will **regenerate** static HTML pages whenever markdown notes change._
