What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **voiced sound**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌvɔɪst ˈsaʊnd/
- **Meaning**: A speech sound produced with vibration of the vocal cords, such as /b/, /d/, /ɡ/, /v/, /z/ and all vowels (âm hữu thanh có rung dây thanh quản).
- **Collocations**:
  - `produce a voiced sound`
  - `voiced sound vibration`
  - `voiced vs voiceless sounds`
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Placing fingers on your throat confirms the vocal cord vibration of a **voiced sound**._
