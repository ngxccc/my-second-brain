What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **outlive**?

---

- **Type**: Verb (Word Family)
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌaʊtˈlɪv/
- **Meaning**: To live, survive, or remain valid longer than someone or something else; in systems: when a variable or reference exists beyond the execution scope of its creator (tồn tại lâu hơn, sống lâu hơn phạm vi).
- **Word Family**:
  - **Verb**: `outlive` (/ˌaʊtˈlɪv/ - tồn tại lâu hơn)
  - **Noun**: `N/A`
  - **Adjective**: `N/A`
  - **Adverb**: `N/A`
- **Collocations**:
  - `outlive the function scope` (sống lâu hơn phạm vi hàm)
  - `outlive the stack frame` (tồn tại lâu hơn khung ngăn xếp)
  - `outlive its usefulness` (không còn hữu dụng, lỗi thời)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Verb: If a pointer variable might **outlive** the stack frame where it was allocated, the compiler escapes it to the heap._
  - _Verb: Good architectural patterns **outlive** individual framework trends._
