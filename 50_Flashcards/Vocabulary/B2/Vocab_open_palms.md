What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **open palms**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈoʊ.pən ˈpɑːmz/
- **Meaning**: A non-verbal body language gesture where the hands are displayed openly toward the audience, signaling transparency, honesty, and confidence (lòng bàn tay mở, cử chỉ cởi mở khi thuyết trình).
- **Collocations**:
  - `gesture with open palms` (ra hiệu bằng lòng bàn tay mở)
  - `open palms presentation posture` (tư thế thuyết trình lòng bàn tay mở)
  - `signal trust with open palms` (phát tín hiệu tin cậy bằng bàn tay mở)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Presenting with **open palms** inside the open box zone builds immediate rapport with the interviewer._
  - _Avoid clenching your fists; using **open palms** makes your technical explanations appear more confident._
