What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **language retention**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈlæŋ.ɡwɪdʒ rɪˌten.ʃən/
- **Meaning**: The cognitive ability to retain vocabulary, grammar patterns, and speaking competence in long-term memory without decay (sự ghi nhớ và duy trì ngôn ngữ lâu dài).
- **Collocations**:
  - `maximize language retention` (tối đa hóa khả năng duy trì ngôn ngữ)
  - `long-term language retention` (ghi nhớ ngôn ngữ trong dài hạn)
  - `spaced repetition for language retention` (lặp lại ngắt quãng để duy trì ngôn ngữ)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Spaced repetition systems dramatically improve **language retention** compared to cramming._
  - _Active conversational output is essential for sustaining long-term **language retention**._
