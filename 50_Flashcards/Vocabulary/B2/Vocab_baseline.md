What is the Word Family Matrix, types, pronunciation, and usage of **baseline**?

---

- **Type**: Noun
- **Pronunciation**: /ˈbeɪs.laɪn/
- **Meaning**: A clearly defined starting point, standard, or benchmark used for comparison and measuring future performance (mốc cơ sở, đường chuẩn đối sánh).
- **Collocations**:
  - `performance baseline` (mốc chuẩn hiệu năng)
  - `establish a baseline` (thiết lập một mốc đối sánh)
  - `compare against the baseline` (so sánh đối chiếu với mốc chuẩn)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Before optimizing database queries, run load tests to establish a clear latency **baseline**._
  - _The initial benchmarks provide a **baseline** to verify that the cache refactoring reduced memory consumption._
