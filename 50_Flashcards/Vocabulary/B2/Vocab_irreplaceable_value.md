What is the definition, type, pronunciation, and usage of the term **irreplaceable value**?

---

- **Type**: Noun Phrase
- **Pronunciation**: /ˌɪr.əˈpleɪ.sə.bəl ˈvæl.juː/
- **Meaning**: Unique, highly differentiated worth or expertise that cannot easily be duplicated, automated, or substituted by others (giá trị không thể thay thế).
- **Collocations**:
  - `create irreplaceable value` (tạo ra giá trị không thể thay thế)
  - `provide irreplaceable value to the team` (đem lại giá trị độc nhất cho đội ngũ)
  - `deliver irreplaceable value` (mang lại giá trị khó thay thế)
- **Concrete Examples**:
  - _Senior engineers provide **irreplaceable value** through architectural judgment and system trade-off analysis._
  - _First-principles problem-solving skills build **irreplaceable value** in an era of AI code generators._
